Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy describes how the "Kesit Mafyasi" app (the "App") handles information. The App is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @kesitmafyasi to upload the channel's own edited videos.

1. Information we access

When the account owner logs in with TikTok, the App receives an access token and basic account information (such as the account's open ID, display name and avatar) for the @kesitmafyasi account, together with the permissions the owner approves (for example, uploading videos).

2. How we use it

The information is used only to upload videos and captions to the @kesitmafyasi account and to check the status of those uploads. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the account owner's own computer. We do not run a server that collects or stores TikTok user data.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok, as required to upload videos.

5. Other users

The App is not offered to the public and does not collect information from viewers or other TikTok users.

6. Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services, or by deleting the stored token from the owner's computer.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to @kesitmafyasi on TikTok.
